Our Mission

DepEd NCR
Procurement Unit

Dedicated to serving our schools and divisions across the region, we ensure that every learning resource and service is efficiently provided to support our students and educators.

Transparent & Ethical Practices

Guided by RA No. 9184 and the New Government Procurement Act (RA No. 12009) for fair, accountable, and value-driven public service.

Reliable Stakeholder Support

Providing responsive guidance and streamlined processes for partner offices and suppliers.

Efficient Public Service

Continuously innovating our systems for fast, hassle-free procurement supporting quality education.
